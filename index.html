Questions

The things people ask.

How far in advance should we book?

Sooner is better, particularly for spring and fall Saturdays, which tend to go early. That said, it is always worth asking about a close in date. Sometimes the calendar cooperates.

Do you cover the ceremony as well as the reception?

Yes, and most weddings do it that way. Ceremony, cocktail hour and reception can all be covered, and the lineup can change between them. A solo ceremony into a full band reception is a common shape.

Can you learn our first dance song?

One special request comes learned free with every booking. If you have a longer list of songs that matter to you, send it over and we will talk about it.

What do you need from us at the venue?

Power within reach of where we set up, and enough room for the lineup you booked. For outdoor events we need cover from sun and rain, since the gear does not care for either. If you are unsure whether your space works, send a photo and I will tell you straight.

Is travel extra?

No. Travel is already inside the number I quote you, mileage and lodging included where a date needs it. Nothing gets added afterwards. West Texas dates often cost less than people expect, because I am out that way regularly anyway.

What happens during the breaks?

Music keeps playing through our sound system, so the room never drops into silence between sets.

What kind of country are we talking about?

Originals, the classics, and a lot of country that never got radio play. It leans traditional rather than modern pop country. If you want a feel for it, the video above is a fair sample.

How do we hold the date?

A simple agreement and a deposit. Once that is in, the date is yours and I take it off the calendar for everyone else.
