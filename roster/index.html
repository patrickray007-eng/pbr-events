The slot this fills

The country act your roster is missing.

Most Houston rosters run deep on Top 40 and variety party bands and thin on real Texas country. When a client asks for it, there is usually nothing to send. That is the slot I fill.

Western swing, classic country, Tejano and bluegrass, weighted toward songs a room recognizes, from Hank and Merle through Turnpike and Charley Crockett. Originals are in the set without leading it. Fifteen years gigging, roughly 10,000 miles toured in 2026, and a record cut live in a 150 year old church in the Berkshires. Recent rooms include The Rustic, Truck Yard, Shady Acres Saloon, and Skinny Dennis in Brooklyn.

The other thing worth knowing is that the lineup is not fixed. Solo or duo for a ceremony and cocktail hour, the same players through dinner with the volume down, then the full band for the dance floor. One booking, one load in, three line items covered, and it stages smaller than a party band.

Questions buyers ask

The short answers.

Does the act bring its own sound and lights?

Yes, every configuration from solo up. The rate covers a sound system sized to the room, stage lighting, and someone running it. There is no production vendor to add and no line item that shows up later.

Can one booking cover ceremony through dance floor?

Yes, and that is the usual shape. Solo or duo for the ceremony and cocktail hour, the same players through dinner with the volume down, then the full band once the floor opens. One load in. If the ceremony sits at a separate location or needs its own PA, that is the $400 line in the adjustments table.

Will a country act fill a corporate dance floor?

The two full band clips above are a Brooklyn two step night with the floor full for an original and for a Clint Black cover. The set leans on songs a room recognizes, and the do not play list is yours.

What size does the full band stage at?

Five to six pieces with fiddle and steel, which stages smaller than a Top 40 party band carrying horns. Stage plot and input list on request for whichever configuration you are quoting.

How does travel work outside Houston?

Inside 30 miles of downtown it is in the rate. Past that it is $2.50 per mile, so it is arithmetic you can do rather than a call you have to make. Send me the venue and I will put the number in the quote for you.

What else can you send for a submission?

A longer song list, a stage plot and input list, photos, or additional video. Tell me what your intake needs and I will get it over.
